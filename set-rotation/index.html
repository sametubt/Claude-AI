An RRG reflects relative price and momentum — not fund flow. It says nothing about net money moving into or out of a group. Use it alongside fundamentals, market conditions and your own risk management. This is not investment advice. The numbers on screen right now are a synthetic demo series, generated from a fixed seed to show how the dashboard behaves — they are not real market data. Use the CSV button to load your own closing prices.

How to read it

RS-Ratio
How strong a group is against the benchmark, normalised so that 100 is the market. Above 100 means it is outperforming the benchmark trend.
RS-Momentum
The rate of change of RS-Ratio — whether that strength is still building or fading. Above 100 means it is still accelerating.
↗ Leading
Stronger than the market, with momentum still behind it.
↘ Weakening
Still stronger than the market, but momentum is fading — often where a rotation out begins.
↙ Lagging
Weaker than the market, with momentum still negative.
↖ Improving
Still weaker than the market, but momentum has turned positive — often where a rotation in begins.
Rotation
Groups tend to travel clockwise: Improving → Leading → Weakening → Lagging. That is a tendency, not a rule.
Tail
The path a group took over the preceding periods. Longer, straighter tails mean a more persistent move.
